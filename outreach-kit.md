# ValuedHR Outreach Kit — Employee Relations + HR Fire Drill

Goal: booked Fire Drills ($495) and discovery calls. Send 20–30 personalized messages/day.

## Who to target

- US small businesses, **10–50 employees** — big enough to have people problems, too small for an HR department.
- Highest-pain industries: home services/trades, medical & dental practices, restaurants/hospitality groups, agencies, light manufacturing, nonprofits.
- The buyer: **owner, GM, or office manager** — whoever gets pulled into people drama.
- Trigger signals (find on LinkedIn/Indeed/Google): actively hiring (growth = friction), bad Glassdoor/Indeed reviews mentioning management, recent leadership change, multiple reposted job ads (turnover).

## Objection handling

- **"We can't afford HR."** — You are not hiring HR. It is $495 once, for the problem on your desk today. Compare that to 1–2× salary to replace whoever quits over it.
- **"I'll handle it myself."** — Most owners do, eventually. The Fire Drill means you handle it this week, with scripts, instead of improvising a conversation that can create legal exposure.
- **"Is this legal advice?"** — No, and I will tell you plainly if your situation needs an employment lawyer. That answer alone is worth the fee.
- **"Why not just a free call?"** — Take the free call. The Fire Drill is for when you need the answer in 48 hours, not a proposal in a week.

## Compliance (required for cold email)

Every cold email must include your physical business address and a working opt-out line (CAN-SPAM). Add this footer to all three emails:

> ValuedHR Business Services · Wake Forest, NC {street address}
> If you would rather not hear from me, reply "no thanks" and I will not email again.

Honor opt-outs immediately. Sending from michelle.mendez@valuedhr.com (not a bulk tool) at 20–30/day keeps deliverability and authenticity high.
