In short: We use personal information to answer inquiries, provide HR and coaching services, operate our website and chat, process purchases, deliver products, support customers, and improve our services. We do not ask you to submit sensitive employee, candidate, medical, accommodation, performance, or payment-card information through ordinary website forms or chat.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information handled by ValuedHR Business Services ("ValuedHR," "we," "us," or "our") through valuedhr.com, related ValuedHR web pages and product sites, website forms and chat, purchases, customer support, and our HR consulting, recruiting, coaching, educational, and digital-product services.

This policy does not govern a third party's independent website or service. When you follow a link to another provider, that provider's terms and privacy notice apply.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact and business details, such as your name, work email, telephone number, company, website, role, service region, and the service or partnership opportunity that interests you.
  • Inquiry and service information, including messages, consultation requests, scheduling details, project communications, feedback, and support requests.
  • Purchase information, such as the product purchased, transaction status, amount, receipt details, and the contact information needed to deliver and support the purchase. Payment-card details are collected and processed by Stripe rather than stored by ValuedHR.
  • Marketing preferences, including newsletter requests, consent choices, and unsubscribe or do-not-contact requests.
  • Partner information, such as professional qualifications, practice details, ideal clients, geographic coverage, and partnership interests.

Information collected automatically

When you use our website, we and our service providers may receive device and usage data such as IP address, browser and device type, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, timestamps, chat activity, and interactions with site content. These technologies may use cookies, local storage, pixels, session-replay or analytics tools, and similar identifiers.

Information from other sources

We may receive business contact information from referrals, professional networks, public business sources, service providers, and platforms you use to communicate with us. We use that information only for legitimate business purposes and subject to applicable law.

Please protect workplace data. Do not submit confidential employee or candidate records, medical or accommodation information, Social Security numbers, payment-card numbers, investigation details, or other highly sensitive information through public forms or website chat. Contact us first so we can identify an appropriate method.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to inquiries, schedule conversations, and recommend relevant services;
  • provide consulting, recruiting, coaching, educational, digital-product, and customer-support services;
  • process purchases, confirm transactions, deliver products, and administer refunds or disputes;
  • evaluate potential partners and manage business relationships;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, analyze, and improve our websites and services;
  • send requested updates and, where permitted, relevant marketing communications;
  • maintain records, enforce agreements, prevent fraud or misuse, and protect rights and safety; and
  • comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website and service providers may use technologies that are necessary for site operation, remember preferences, support chat, measure website use, diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and understand which content or campaigns are useful.

You can limit cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect chat, forms, preferences, or other site functions. You may also use privacy controls offered by the relevant service provider. Browser-based opt-out signals are handled where required by applicable law and supported by our systems.

6. Retention and security

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business and transaction records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and satisfying legal, tax, accounting, and compliance obligations. Retention periods vary based on the type of information and our relationship with you.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. No internet transmission, email, website, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use care when deciding what information to send online.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You may unsubscribe from marketing email using the link in the message or by contacting us. Even after opting out of marketing, we may send transactional or service-related communications, such as purchase receipts, product delivery, appointment information, and support responses.

Depending on where you live and subject to applicable exceptions, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information; to object to or restrict some processing; or to appeal our response. We may need to verify your identity and authority before completing a request.

To submit a request, email support@valuedhr.com with the subject line “Privacy Request.” You may use an authorized agent where applicable. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

8. Children's privacy

ValuedHR's websites and services are intended for businesses and adults, not children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, providers, or legal obligations. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use previously collected information, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent when required.
