Frequently asked questions

Before you book

Clear expectations make urgent work calmer. Here are the questions clients most often ask.

When does the 48-hour turnaround begin?

The 48-hour action-plan window begins after your completed 60-minute working session, once we have the context needed to prepare useful guidance. It does not begin at checkout or when the intake is submitted.

What happens immediately after I pay?

Stripe redirects you to a short intake form. After submitting it, you choose a time for your private working session. The intake asks for high-level operational context only—please do not include employee names or sensitive personal information.

Is this legal advice?

No. ValuedHR provides HR consulting, not legal advice, and the engagement does not create an attorney-client relationship or legal privilege. When the situation calls for an employment attorney, we will tell you directly and recommend that you seek qualified counsel.

Can I bring more than one problem to the session?

The Fire Drill is designed around one defined problem. We can consider related facts and consequences, but unrelated issues may require a separate engagement. If the situation reveals a broader pattern, we will explain the most efficient next option.

Will you tell me whether I should fire someone?

We will help you assess the HR process, available facts, documentation, alternatives, communication needs, and reasons to involve legal counsel. The final employment decision remains yours, and we do not provide a legal determination about whether a termination is lawful.

Can ValuedHR speak to the employee or attend the meeting?

Direct employee contact, meeting attendance, investigations, and ongoing case management are outside the Fire Drill’s fixed scope. If hands-on support is appropriate, we can discuss a separate project or ongoing HR engagement.

How private is the session?

We handle the information you share as private business information and limit collection to what is reasonably needed to provide the service. HR consulting is not protected by attorney-client privilege. Do not submit unnecessary medical, financial, identity, or similarly sensitive information through the intake form.

What does the week of email support include?

It covers reasonable clarification questions about the delivered plan as you begin using it. It does not add new issues, rewrite the scope, provide real-time emergency support, or include ongoing case management.

What if the issue is urgent or someone may be in danger?

The HR Fire Drill is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, threats of harm, or a medical emergency, contact the appropriate emergency services and follow your organization’s safety procedures. Seek qualified legal counsel when urgent legal action may be required.

What if I need more support afterward?

We will recommend the right next step based on the actual scope—such as a defined project, ongoing fractional HR support, or another specialist. If you continue into a ValuedHR retainer, the $495 Fire Drill fee is credited toward your first month.
