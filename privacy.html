Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Information we collect

We collect information you voluntarily submit, including your name, email address, company, role, approximate team size, work location, service interest, timeline, and high-level description of the support you need. Please do not submit employee names, medical or financial information, identity numbers, passwords, or confidential documents through our website forms. With consent, analytics and chat providers may collect device, usage, and interaction information.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare for and provide requested services, manage newsletter subscriptions, understand site performance, and protect the website from abuse. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

We use hosting, customer-relationship management, scheduling, payment, email, analytics, and chat providers—including Hostinger, Zoho, Reclaim, and Stripe—to operate the website and deliver services. These providers process information under their own privacy terms.

Choices and retention

You can decline optional analytics through the cookie banner. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information. We retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and applicable legal obligations.
